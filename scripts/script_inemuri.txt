Did you know that falling asleep at your desk in Japan isn't lazy? It's actually respected!
This is called Inemuri.
It roughly translates to "sleeping while present."
In Japanese corporate culture, it proves you've been working so exhaustively that you simply couldn't stay awake anymore.
But there is a catch.
You must remain upright to show you're still socially engaged.
No beds, no stretching out!
Just pure dedication.
Would you survive this rule?
