## Accuracy note

The physical mechanism and existence of the nasal cycle are well established. Its complete evolutionary and physiological purpose remains debated, so the script presents air conditioning, mucosal recovery, clearance, defense, and olfactory sampling as supported hypotheses and advantages rather than a single proven explanation.
