284: At least in some cases, the location formed part of the meaning.
285: Dating the paintings creates another challenge.
286: Many people imagine archaeologists simply testing the pigment and receiving an exact year.
287: Often, it is much harder.
288: Charcoal can sometimes be radiocarbon dated because it contains carbon from once-living material.
289: But mineral pigments such as ochre contain no organic carbon to test.
290: Researchers may instead date mineral crusts that formed above or below an image.
291: If a calcium carbonate layer grew on top of a painting,
292: the painting must be older than that layer.
293: This produces a minimum age, not necessarily the exact date of creation.
294: New techniques can push the known age of an image further into the past without changing the image itself.

=== 375 - 394 ===
